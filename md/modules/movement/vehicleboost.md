## VehicleBoost

VehicleBoost throws you forward and up when you get off a ride, like a boat, minecart, horse or pig. You fly in the direction you are looking. Use it to quickly catch up to someone or get away.

**Category:** Movement
**Enabled by default:** No

### Settings

| Setting | Type | Default | Range | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| HorizontalSpeed | Decimal | 2.0 | 0.1..10.0 | How hard you are thrown forward. |
| VerticalSpeed | Decimal | 1.0 | 0.1..5.0 | How hard you are thrown up. |
