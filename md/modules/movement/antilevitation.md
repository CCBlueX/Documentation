## AntiLevitation

AntiLevitation stops Levitation and Slow Falling from moving you. Levitation won't lift you into the air and Slow Falling won't slow your fall. The effect icons still show.

**Category:** Movement
**Enabled by default:** No

### Settings

This module has no settings.
