## NoPose

NoPose stops you from going into the flat swimming pose and lets you pick how tall you are while sneaking. Use it on servers made for older Minecraft versions, where these poses can cause problems.

**Category:** Movement
**Enabled by default:** No

### Settings

| Setting | Type | Default | Range | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| NoSwim | Toggle | false |  | Keeps you upright when you sprint in water, so you never swim lying flat. |
| SneakHeight | Choice | 1.15 | 1.8, 1.9, 1.15 | How tall you are while sneaking. 1.8 keeps your full height, 1.9 makes you a little shorter, 1.15 is the normal height of today's Minecraft. Pick the version the server is made for. |
