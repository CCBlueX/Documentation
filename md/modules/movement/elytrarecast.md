## ElytraRecast

ElytraRecast opens your elytra again by itself while you hold the jump key. You don't have to time a new jump press when your glide stops.

It only works when you wear an elytra that won't break on its next use. It does nothing while you creative-fly, ride something, climb, swim or have Levitation.

**Category:** Movement
**Enabled by default:** No

### Settings

This module has no settings.
