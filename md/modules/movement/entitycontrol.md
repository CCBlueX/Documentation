## EntityControl

EntityControl lets you ride and steer animals like pigs and horses without a saddle. It can also let mounts jump when you press the jump key.

**Category:** Movement
**Enabled by default:** No

### Settings

| Setting | Type | Default | Range | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Enforce | Multi-Select | Saddled, JumpStrength | Saddled, JumpStrength | Saddled: the mount acts as if it has a saddle, so you can steer it. JumpStrength: mounts that can jump will jump when you press jump. |
