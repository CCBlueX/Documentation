## HighJump

HighJump makes your jumps higher. Use it to get onto ledges and over walls a normal jump can't reach.

**Category:** Movement
**Enabled by default:** No

### Settings

| Setting | Type | Default | Range | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mode | Mode Selector | Vanilla | Vanilla, Vulcan-2.7.5 | Vanilla: a stronger jump, for servers without an anti-cheat. Vulcan-2.7.5: made for the Vulcan anti-cheat. |
| Mode → [Vulcan-2.7.5] → Glide | Toggle | false |  | Fall slowly after the jump. |
| Motion | Decimal | 0.8 | 0.2..10.0 | How strong the jump is. Higher means higher. |
