## NoJumpDelay

NoJumpDelay removes the short wait between jumps, so you jump again right away when you land while holding the jump key. It goes well with [Speed](/docs/modules/movement/speed) and [Parkour](/docs/modules/movement/parkour).

**Category:** Movement
**Enabled by default:** No

### Settings

This module has no settings.
