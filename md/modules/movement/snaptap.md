## SnapTap

SnapTap makes the key you pressed last win when you hold two opposite walk keys. Normally holding A and D together makes you stop; with SnapTap you go the way of the newer key. The same goes for W and S. This makes quick dodges in fights feel faster.

**Category:** Movement
**Enabled by default:** No

### Settings

This module has no settings.
