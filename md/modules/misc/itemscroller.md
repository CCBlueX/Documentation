## ItemScroller

ItemScroller moves many items at once. In an inventory, hold Shift and the left mouse button and drag your mouse over the slots. Each item you pass over is moved, like a Shift-click on every slot.

**Category:** Misc
**Enabled by default:** No

### Settings

| Setting | Type | Default | Range | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ClickMode | Choice | QuickMove | QuickMove | How items are moved. QuickMove works like Shift-click. |
| Delay | Integer Range | 2..3 | 0..20 ticks | Wait between moves, in ticks (20 per second). |
