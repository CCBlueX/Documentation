## AutoBreak

AutoBreak mines for you. Look at a block and it starts breaking it, as if you were holding the attack button. It stops when you look away or the block is gone. Turn it on when you have a lot of mining to do and don't want to hold the button.

To break blocks faster, use it together with [FastBreak](/docs/modules/world/fastbreak) or [NoSlowBreak](/docs/modules/world/noslowbreak).

**Category:** Player
**Enabled by default:** No

### Settings

This module has no settings.
