## PotionSpoof

PotionSpoof gives you potion effects without drinking any potions. Turn on the effects you want and pick their level, for example Night Vision to see in the dark or Jump Boost to jump higher. The effects go away when you turn the module off.

The effects only exist in your own game. The server doesn't know about them, so effects it would have to apply, like Strength making your hits stronger, often don't work on servers.

**Category:** Player
**Enabled by default:** No
