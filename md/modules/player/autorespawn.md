## AutoRespawn

AutoRespawn presses the Respawn button for you as soon as you die, so you are back in the game right away. This helps on PvP servers where you want to get back to your team or your items fast.

**Category:** Player
**Enabled by default:** No

### Settings

| Setting | Type | Default | Range | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Delay | Integer | 0 | 0..20 ticks | How long to wait on the death screen before respawning, in ticks (20 per second). Raise it if respawning right away causes problems on a server. |
