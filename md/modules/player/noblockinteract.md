## NoBlockInteract

NoBlockInteract lets you use the item in your hand while looking at a block you could open, like a chest, door or crafting table. Normally right-clicking opens the block. With this module the client sneaks for a moment, so your food, potion or bow gets used instead.

It only steps in when you are not already sneaking and you hold an item that can be used.

**Category:** Player
**Enabled by default:** No

### Settings

This module has no settings.
