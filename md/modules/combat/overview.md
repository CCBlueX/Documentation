## Combat Modules

Combat modules help you fight other players and mobs. They can attack, aim, block or put on armor for you.

There are **29** modules in this category:

- [Aimbot](/docs/modules/combat/aimbot): Turns your view toward enemies near you.
- [AutoArmor](/docs/modules/combat/autoarmor): Puts on the best armor in your inventory.
- [AutoBow](/docs/modules/combat/autobow): Aims and shoots your bow for you.
- [AutoClicker](/docs/modules/combat/autoclicker): Clicks for you while you hold a mouse button.
- [AutoDodge](/docs/modules/combat/autododge): Moves you out of the way of arrows and tridents.
- [AutoLeave](/docs/modules/combat/autoleave): Leaves the server when your health gets low.
- [AutoPearl](/docs/modules/combat/autopearl): Throws an ender pearl after enemies who pearl away.
- [AutoRod](/docs/modules/combat/autorod): Throws a fishing rod at enemies close to you.
- [AutoShoot](/docs/modules/combat/autoshoot): Throws eggs and snowballs at enemies.
- [AutoWeapon](/docs/modules/combat/autoweapon): Switches to your best weapon when you hit.
- [Backtrack](/docs/modules/combat/backtrack): Shows enemies a little behind where they are, so you have more time to hit.
- [Criticals](/docs/modules/combat/criticals): Makes more of your hits critical hits.
- [CrystalAura](/docs/modules/combat/crystalaura): Places and blows up end crystals next to enemies.
- [DroneControl](/docs/modules/combat/dronecontrol): Shoots your bow from a camera that flies above you.
- [ElytraTarget](/docs/modules/combat/elytratarget): Chases players while you fly with an elytra.
- [FakeLag](/docs/modules/combat/fakelag): Makes you look laggy so enemies miss you.
- [Hitbox](/docs/modules/combat/hitbox): Makes enemies easier to hit.
- [KeepSprint](/docs/modules/combat/keepsprint): Keeps you sprinting when you hit.
- [KillAura](/docs/modules/combat/killaura): Attacks enemies near you by itself.
- [MaceKill](/docs/modules/combat/macekill): Makes Mace hits do a lot more damage.
- [NoMissCooldown](/docs/modules/combat/nomisscooldown): Removes the wait after a missed swing.
- [ProjectileAimbot](/docs/modules/combat/projectileaimbot): Aims what you throw or shoot at enemies.
- [SpearKill](/docs/modules/combat/spearkill): Dashes you to far away enemies with a charged spear.
- [SuperKnockback](/docs/modules/combat/superknockback): Makes your hits push enemies further.
- [SwordBlock](/docs/modules/combat/swordblock): Lets you block with a sword like in 1.8.
- [TickBase](/docs/modules/combat/tickbase): Gets you to enemies a little early so you hit first.
- [TpAura](/docs/modules/combat/tpaura): Teleports you to enemies, hits them and takes you back.
- [TriggerBot](/docs/modules/combat/triggerbot): Attacks when your crosshair is on an enemy.
- [Velocity](/docs/modules/combat/velocity): Changes how far you get knocked back.
