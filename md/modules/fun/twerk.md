## Twerk

Twerk makes you sneak and stand up again over and over by itself.

**Category:** Fun
**Enabled by default:** No

### Settings

| Setting | Type | Default | Range | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Delay | Integer | 2 | 1..20 | How many ticks (20 per second) you stay down and up each time. Lower is faster. |
