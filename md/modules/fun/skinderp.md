## SkinDerp

SkinDerp makes the outer layers of your skin (hat, jacket, sleeves, pants and cape) blink on and off. Your skin needs these layers for it to show. When you turn it off, your skin goes back to how it was.

**Category:** Fun
**Enabled by default:** No

### Settings

| Setting | Type | Default | Range | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sync | Toggle | false |  | On: all picked parts blink together. Off: each part turns on or off at random. |
| Delay | Integer | 0 | 0..20 ticks | How long to wait between blinks, in ticks (20 per second). |
| Parts | Multi-Select | Hat, Jacket, LeftPants, RightPants, LeftSleeve, RightSleeve, Cape | Hat, Jacket, LeftPants, RightPants, LeftSleeve, RightSleeve, Cape | Which parts of your skin blink. |
