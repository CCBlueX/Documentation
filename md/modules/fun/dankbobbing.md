## DankBobbing

DankBobbing makes the way your screen and hand sway while you walk much bigger and sillier. Only you see it. To turn the sway off instead, use [NoBob](/docs/modules/render/nobob).

**Category:** Fun
**Enabled by default:** No

### Settings

| Setting | Type | Default | Range | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Motion | Decimal | 5.0 | 1.0..50.0 | How strong the sway is. |
