## MurderMystery

MurderMystery helps you in the Murder Mystery minigame, for example on Hypixel. It finds the murderer and marks them in red. Players with a bow, like the detective, are marked in blue. Bows lying on the ground get a box around them.

Pick the **Mode** that matches the game you are playing. **Classic** spots the player who takes out a sword and tells you in chat. **Infection** finds the first infected player and players with bows. **Assassination** reads your contract map to find your target and uses sounds to find the player hunting you.

**Category:** Render
**Enabled by default:** No

### Settings

| Setting | Type | Default | Range | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SetTeamPrefix | Toggle | true | | Puts `[MURD]` or `[BOW]` in front of the marked players' names. |
| Mode | Mode Selector | Classic | Classic, Infection, Assassination | Which Murder Mystery game you are playing. |
