## FullBright

FullBright makes the world bright, so you can see in caves, underwater and at night without torches.

**Gamma** turns the game's brightness up past its normal limit. **NightVision** gives you the Night Vision effect instead.

**Category:** Render
**Enabled by default:** No

### Settings

| Setting | Type | Default | Range | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mode | Mode Selector | Gamma | Gamma, NightVision | How the world is made brighter. |
| Mode → Gamma → Brightness | Integer | 15 | 1 to 15 | How bright the world gets. Higher is brighter. |
