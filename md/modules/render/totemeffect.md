## TotemEffect

TotemEffect draws a visual effect for the Totem of Undying. Like other effect modules such as [JumpEffect](/docs/modules/render/jumpeffect) and [HitFX](/docs/modules/render/hitfx), it is purely cosmetic and does not change how the totem itself behaves.

**Category:** Render
