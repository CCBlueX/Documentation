## MobOwners

MobOwners shows the name of the owner above tamed animals like wolves, cats and horses. You can see if a pet belongs to you, a friend or someone else.

Turn on **Projectiles** to also see who shot an arrow or threw a trident. If the owner is not on the server, the name can take a moment to load and shows "Loading..." until then.

**Category:** Render
**Enabled by default:** No

### Settings

| Setting | Type | Default | Range | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Projectiles | Toggle | false | | Also shows the owner above arrows, tridents and other projectiles. |
