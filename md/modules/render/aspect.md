## Aspect

Aspect stretches or squeezes your view from side to side without changing your FOV. 100% is the normal view. Lower values squeeze the picture, higher values stretch it wider.

**Category:** Render
**Enabled by default:** No

### Settings

| Setting | Type | Default | Range | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ratio | Integer | 100% | 1..300% | How much the view is stretched sideways. 100% is normal. |
