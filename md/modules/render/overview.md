## Render Modules

Render modules change what you see on your screen. They can show players and blocks through walls, add effects, change the camera or tweak the HUD. Other players never see these changes.

There are **60** modules in this category:

- [AntiBlind](/docs/modules/render/antiblind): hides screen effects that block your view, like blindness or pumpkin overlays.
- [Aspect](/docs/modules/render/aspect): stretches or squeezes the picture sideways.
- [AutoF5](/docs/modules/render/autof5): switches to third person when you open your inventory or a chest.
- [BedPlates](/docs/modules/render/bedplates): shows which blocks are protecting a bed.
- [BetterChat](/docs/modules/render/betterchat): adds extra options to the chat.
- [BetterInventory](/docs/modules/render/betterinventory): adds extra visual help in your inventory.
- [BetterTab](/docs/modules/render/bettertab): improves the player list you see when holding Tab.
- [BetterTitle](/docs/modules/render/bettertitle): changes the big title and subtitle texts and can translate them.
- [BlockESP](/docs/modules/render/blockesp): shows blocks you pick through walls.
- [BlockOutline](/docs/modules/render/blockoutline): changes the outline around the block you are looking at.
- [Breadcrumbs](/docs/modules/render/breadcrumbs): draws a trail behind you.
- [CameraClip](/docs/modules/render/cameraclip): lets the third person camera go through walls.
- [Chams](/docs/modules/render/chams): shows players through walls.
- [ClickGUI](/docs/modules/render/clickgui): opens the menu where you turn modules on and change their settings.
- [CombineMobs](/docs/modules/render/combinemobs): draws crowded mobs of the same kind as one.
- [Crosshair](/docs/modules/render/crosshair): changes how your crosshair looks.
- [CrystalView](/docs/modules/render/crystalview): changes how end crystals look and move.
- [CustomAmbience](/docs/modules/render/customambience): changes the time of day, weather and fog you see.
- [DamageParticles](/docs/modules/render/damageparticles): shows numbers when mobs and players lose or gain health.
- [Debug](/docs/modules/render/debug): tools for the client's developers.
- [ESP](/docs/modules/render/esp): highlights players and mobs so you can see them through walls.
- [FreeCam](/docs/modules/render/freecam): lets you fly the camera away from your body.
- [FreeLook](/docs/modules/render/freelook): lets you look around your character without turning it.
- [FullBright](/docs/modules/render/fullbright): makes dark places bright.
- [HUD](/docs/modules/render/hud): shows the client's on-screen overlay.
- [Hats](/docs/modules/render/hats): puts a hat on players' heads.
- [HitFX](/docs/modules/render/hitfx): plays an effect when you hit something.
- [HoleESP](/docs/modules/render/holeesp): shows safe holes for crystal PvP.
- [ItemChams](/docs/modules/render/itemchams): adds effects to the items in your hands.
- [ItemESP](/docs/modules/render/itemesp): shows dropped items through walls.
- [ItemTags](/docs/modules/render/itemtags): shows icons and amounts above dropped items.
- [JumpEffect](/docs/modules/render/jumpeffect): shows an effect under your feet when you jump.
- [LogoffSpot](/docs/modules/render/logoffspot): shows where other players left the game.
- [MobOwners](/docs/modules/render/mobowners): shows which player owns a pet or shot a projectile.
- [MurderMystery](/docs/modules/render/murdermystery): tells you who the murderer is in Murder Mystery.
- [Nametags](/docs/modules/render/nametags): makes name tags easier to read and adds extra information.
- [NewChunks](/docs/modules/render/newchunks): marks chunks that were probably just generated.
- [NoBob](/docs/modules/render/nobob): stops the screen from bobbing while you walk.
- [NoFOV](/docs/modules/render/nofov): stops your field of view from changing while you move.
- [NoHurtCam](/docs/modules/render/nohurtcam): stops the screen shake when you get hurt.
- [NoSwing](/docs/modules/render/noswing): hides your hand swing.
- [Particles](/docs/modules/render/particles): shows particles when you attack.
- [PotionFX](/docs/modules/render/potionfx): draws custom effects for potions.
- [ProphuntESP](/docs/modules/render/prophuntesp): shows the hidden props in Prop Hunt.
- [ProtectionZones](/docs/modules/render/protectionzones): shows areas guarded by protection blocks and good spots to place them.
- [QuickPerspectiveSwap](/docs/modules/render/quickperspectiveswap): switches the camera view while you hold a key.
- [Radar](/docs/modules/render/radar): points arrows at nearby players and mobs, even when they are off screen.
- [SilentHotbar](/docs/modules/render/silenthotbar): keeps showing your real held item when a module switches slots.
- [SkinChanger](/docs/modules/render/skinchanger): changes your skin on your own screen, for example for videos.
- [SmoothCamera](/docs/modules/render/smoothcamera): makes camera movement smoother.
- [StorageESP](/docs/modules/render/storageesp): shows chests and other storage blocks through walls.
- [TNTTimer](/docs/modules/render/tnttimer): shows lit TNT and when it will explode.
- [TotemEffect](/docs/modules/render/totemeffect): draws an effect when a Totem of Undying is used.
- [Tracers](/docs/modules/render/tracers): draws lines to players and mobs near you.
- [Trajectories](/docs/modules/render/trajectories): shows where arrows and other thrown things will land.
- [TrueSight](/docs/modules/render/truesight): shows invisible players, mobs and blocks.
- [VoidESP](/docs/modules/render/voidesp): marks places where you could fall into the void.
- [Wings](/docs/modules/render/wings): draws wings on your back and on your friends.
- [XRay](/docs/modules/render/xray): shows only the blocks you pick, like ores.
- [Zoom](/docs/modules/render/zoom): zooms your view in.
