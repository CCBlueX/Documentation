## Language

Chooses the language of LiquidBounce's own texts, like menus and chat messages. On Auto, the client uses the same language as your Minecraft. You can also change it with [`.client language set <code>`](/docs/commands/client), and `.client language list` shows the codes.

### Settings

| Setting | Type | Default | Range | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ClientLanguage | Choice | Auto | Auto, English (US), English (Pirate), German, Japanese, Chinese (Simplified), Chinese (Traditional), Russian, Ukrainian, Portuguese (Brazil), Turkish, Dutch (Netherlands), Dutch (Belgium) | The language of the client. Auto follows your Minecraft language. |
